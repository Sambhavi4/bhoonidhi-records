\textbf{The gap} & Bhoonidhi stores every scene's footprint polygon and lets you
ask one question of it: does it intersect mine? No analysis, no processing
endpoint anywhere in ISRO's public stack. \\[4pt]
\textbf{Why it matters} & Measured: \textbf{208 scenes returned where 16 cover
the same ground --- 92\,\% redundant}, 206\,GB ordered where 16\,GB was needed.
Copernicus publishes 146 server-side processes; Bhoonidhi publishes 0. \\[4pt]
\textbf{What was built} & A conformant \textbf{OGC API --- Processes} service
(pygeoapi, shapely): six spatial operations, sync + async execution, a job API,
areas in an equal-area projection. \\[4pt]
\textbf{Evidence} & \textbf{100/100 compliance tests pass.} Every number here is
produced by a script in the repository and saved under \code{docs/evidence/}. \\
\end{tabular}
