\section{Live demonstration}
\label{sec:results}

Base URL \ep{http://localhost:5003}; reproduced by \code{./scripts/demo.sh}.
AOI throughout: \code{[75.3, 13.2, 75.8, 13.9]}, a district-sized window
(4\,191\,km\textsuperscript{2}) over Chikkamagaluru, Karnataka.

\subsection{The gap, closed}

\begin{lstlisting}
curl -X POST localhost:5003/processes/minimal-scene-set/execution \
  -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d '{"inputs":{"aoi":[75.3,13.2,75.8,13.9],"collection":"sentinel-2-l2a"}}'
\end{lstlisting}

\begin{lstlisting}[style=json]
"candidates_considered": 30,  "scenes_selected": 2,  "scenes_avoided": 28,
"achieved_percent": 100.0,
"selection": [
  { "order": 1, "new_area_km2": 2517.58, "cumulative_coverage_fraction": 0.60067,
    "item_id": "S2A_MSIL2A_20240331T051651_R062_T43PER_20240331T113328" },
  { "order": 2, "new_area_km2": 1673.70, "cumulative_coverage_fraction": 1.0,
    "item_id": "S2A_MSIL2A_20240331T051651_R062_T43PEQ_20240331T113919" }
],
"algorithm": "greedy set cover; ... within a ln(n) factor of optimal and not
              guaranteed minimal."
\end{lstlisting}

\begin{keyfinding}{Thirty scenes match the AOI. Two cover it.}
Ordering the other 28 is wasted download, wasted quota and wasted time --- and
nothing in Bhoonidhi's API tells you that. The response also gives the
\emph{coverage curve}: the first scene buys 60.1\,\%, the second completes it,
so a user needing only 60\,\% can stop after one.
\end{keyfinding}

\subsection{The other five processes}

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}l>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}X@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Process} & \textbf{Result} \\
\midrule
\code{aoi-coverage}
  & 4\,191.28\,km\textsuperscript{2} AOI, 100\,\% covered by 30 scenes, 0
    gaps, \code{fully\_covered: true} and \code{gaps: null} --- not a hairline
    sliver (defect 2). Where coverage is partial, \code{gaps} carries the
    remainder as GeoJSON polygons: not a percentage, but \emph{where} the
    holes are. \\
\code{multi-sensor-overlap}
  & optical 30 scenes $\cap$ radar 8 scenes $=$ 4\,191.28\,km\textsuperscript{2},
    100\,\% of the AOI co-covered. One call instead of two searches and a
    manual clip. \\
\code{revisit-density}
  & grid 6$\times$8 @ 10\,km $\rightarrow$ 48 cells, 30 acquisitions; per cell
    min 15, mean 18.75, max 30, none unobserved. Returned as GeoJSON ready to
    render as a heatmap. Revisit is \emph{exactly twice} as dense in some cells
    as others --- a flat list has no spatial distribution in it at all. \\
\code{zonal-statistics}
  & 518\,400 pixels over 478.9\,km\textsuperscript{2}; elevation
    613.7--1439.1\,m, mean 746.4, sd 112.6, with a histogram giving the area in
    each value band. \\
\code{scene-raster-profile}
  & the join: \code{T43PER} 1538.0\,km\textsuperscript{2} at 584--1874\,m
    (mean 752) vs.\ \code{T43PEQ} 801.3\,km\textsuperscript{2} at 650--1890\,m
    (mean 941). Two candidates, genuinely different terrain underneath ---
    impossible to tell apart from a catalogue that answers only \emph{does this
    intersect your box}. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}

\subsection{Asynchronous execution and the job API}

\begin{lstlisting}
$ curl -i -X POST localhost:5003/processes/aoi-coverage/execution \
    -H 'Content-Type: application/json' -H 'Prefer: respond-async' \
    -d '{"inputs":{"aoi":[75.3,13.2,75.8,13.9],"collection":"sentinel-2-l2a"}}'

HTTP/1.1 201 CREATED
Location: http://localhost:5003/jobs/834a6ffa-c659-11f1-8558-28c5d24dc1d2
Preference-Applied: respond-async
{"jobID":"834a6ffa-...","type":"process","status":"accepted"}

$ curl 'localhost:5003/jobs/834a6ffa-...?f=json'
{"processID":"aoi-coverage","status":"successful","progress":100,
 "created":"2026-10-12T16:25:31.100225Z","finished":"2026-10-12T16:25:31.113922Z"}

$ curl -o /dev/null -w '%{http_code}\n' 'localhost:5003/jobs/834a6ffa-.../results?f=json'
200
\end{lstlisting}

The conformant shape in full: 201 Created, a \code{Location} header,
\code{Preference-Applied}, a status document with timestamps, results on
completion. A test walks this whole sequence, not just the 201.

\subsection{Errors, and performance}

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lr@{\qquad\qquad}lr@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Bad request} & \textbf{Response} & \textbf{Process} & \textbf{Time} \\
\midrule
unknown process or job id        & 404 & \code{minimal-scene-set}    & 0.009\,s \\
missing or malformed \code{aoi}  & 400 & \code{aoi-coverage}         & 0.010\,s \\
inverted bbox (min $>$ max)      & 400 & \code{multi-sensor-overlap} & 0.010\,s \\
wrong geometry type              & 400 & \code{revisit-density}      & 0.018\,s \\
\code{target\_fraction} out of range & 400 & \code{zonal-statistics} & 0.074\,s \\
grid finer than the cell cap     & 400 & \code{scene-raster-profile} & 0.585\,s \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Measured end to end over HTTP. The four footprint processes answer in roughly
10\,ms --- which is rather the point: \textbf{the work Bhoonidhi leaves to every
user is work a server does in ten milliseconds.} The async path exists for
conformance and for larger AOIs, not because these workloads need it.
