\subsection{The suite tests the mathematics, not only the protocol}

This is the point worth a reviewer's attention. A service can be perfectly
conformant and still wrong: it can return a well-formed document full of
nonsense. The suite therefore asserts invariants that must hold if the
geometry is being computed correctly.

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.38\textwidth}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}X@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Invariant asserted} & \textbf{Why it would otherwise slip through} \\
\midrule
covered area $+$ gap area $=$ AOI area
  & the two are computed by different code paths (union vs.\ difference);
    agreement is a genuine cross-check \\
\code{fully\_covered} never coexists with a gap geometry
  & caught defect 2 of Section~\ref{sec:defects} \\
intersection area $\le$ \emph{both} operand areas
  & an intersection larger than an operand is impossible; a projection or
    winding-order bug produces exactly that \\
$\sum$ cell areas $=$ AOI area
  & the density grid is built in projected metres and clipped; a gridding
    off-by-one would not sum \\
cumulative coverage is monotonic non-decreasing
  & greedy set cover cannot lose coverage by adding a scene \\
greedy takes the largest gain first
  & asserts the algorithm is actually greedy, not merely ordered \\
AOI area lands in thousands of km\textsuperscript{2}, not fractions
  & the single highest-value test in the suite: it fails loudly the moment
    anything is computed in degrees \\
empty and populated branches share one schema
  & caught defect 3 of Section~\ref{sec:defects} \\
masking genuinely restricts what is read
  & a raster mask that silently no-ops returns plausible-looking statistics
    for the wrong extent \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}

Two of the three defects in Section~\ref{sec:defects} were found by exactly
these invariant tests rather than by inspection, which is the argument for
writing them.

\subsection{What this evidence is not}

Stated so the claim is not overread: this is a self-written suite, not the
OGC CITE certification suite, and OGC API --- Processes is tested here against
the specification's mandatory requirements as implemented by pygeoapi rather
than by an accredited third party. What can fairly be claimed is that every
mandatory resource is present and exercised, the full asynchronous job
lifecycle is walked end to end, error handling is tested, and the numerical
results are checked against invariants that would catch the realistic failure
modes.
