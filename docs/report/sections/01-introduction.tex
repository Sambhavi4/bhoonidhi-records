\section{Introduction}

Bhoonidhi (\url{https://bhoonidhi.nrsc.gov.in/}) is the National Remote Sensing
Centre's public repository for Indian Earth observation data --- roughly 59
published collections across the ResourceSat, Cartosat, Oceansat, EOS and RISAT
missions.

\textbf{What it already does well,} stated first so the argument is fair. Its
Open Data Access API~\cite{bhoonidhiapi} is built on STAC 1.0.0~\cite{stac},
with token authentication, \ep{/data/collections}, a \ep{/data/search} that
accepts GeoJSON \code{intersects}, a \code{bbox}, RFC 3339 \code{datetime} and
CQL2-JSON filters, and \ep{/download} by item id. A developer can authenticate,
search by space and time, and fetch. \textbf{Discovery is solved}, and this
project claims no credit for noticing otherwise.

\textbf{The gap is in computation.} Bhoonidhi stores the footprint polygon of
every scene it holds, and the only question you may ask of that geometry is
\emph{``does it intersect mine?''} There is no union, no difference, no area, no
aggregation, and no processing endpoint anywhere in ISRO's public stack.

The practical consequence is that a user cannot learn \emph{which} of the scenes
a search returns they actually need --- so the rational move, and the observed
one, is to order all of them. Section~\ref{sec:cost} measures what that costs:
\textbf{208 scenes returned where 16 cover the same ground}.

\subsection{What was built}

A conformant \textbf{OGC API --- Processes}~\cite{ogcprocesses} service
exposing six operations over the catalogue's own geometry and the raster
beneath it:

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}l>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}X@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Process} & \textbf{The question it answers} \\
\midrule
\code{minimal-scene-set}    & Which few of the scenes returned do I actually need? \\
\code{aoi-coverage}         & How much of my AOI is covered, and where are the holes? \\
\code{multi-sensor-overlap} & Where do optical and radar co-cover, for fusion? \\
\code{revisit-density}      & How uneven is acquisition density across my AOI? \\
\code{zonal-statistics}     & What are the raster values inside this polygon? \\
\code{scene-raster-profile} & What terrain is under each candidate scene? \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}

The first four read only footprint geometry --- deciding what is worth fetching
\emph{before} anything is downloaded. The last two read pixels.
\code{scene-raster-profile} joins the two. A supporting OGC API ---
Records~\cite{ogcrecords} catalogue ships alongside, giving the processes
discoverable collection ids to point at; the submission rests on the Processes
tier.
